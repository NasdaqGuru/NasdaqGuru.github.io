Structural Risk Register

Risks That Need Governance

  • Decentralized school variation: use controlled flexibility model with approved exception criteria.
  • Metric definition drift: lock KPI dictionary and require owner sign-off.
  • Policy interpretation variance: create common exception taxonomy and Controller escalation thresholds.
  • Capacity constraint: protect implementation time and decide whether backfill is needed.

Best-Practice Operating Rules

  • Red item limit: no more than three active red flags without steering committee escalation.
  • Decision memo standard: every red item needs owner, options, pro/con, risk, and consequence if deferred.
  • Calendar discipline: convert decisions into dated holds, not informal follow-ups.
  • Evidence first: lead with cycle time, exception rate, adoption, and value-at-risk.
