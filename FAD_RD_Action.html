One-Page Interim Controller Authorization Memo
MEMORANDUM
TO:Financial Process Transformation Project Team & P2P Phase 2 Working Group FROM:Office of the Controller, Harvard Financial Administration (FAD) DATE:May 21, 2026 SUBJECT:Interim Authorization of Updated Auto-Approval Thresholds for P2P Phase 2 User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

1. Purpose & Scope

This memorandum grants temporary, interim authority to utilize the revised Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Phase 2 auto-approval thresholds strictly within the non-production User Acceptance Testing (UAT) environments. This temporary authorization ensures that system testing mirrors production logic ahead of the planned August go-live window.

2. Interim Authority Boundaries

  • Authorized threshold configuration: Auto-approval limits may be modified up to the proposed FY2027 efficiency benchmarks within the UAT tenant only.
  • Duration: This interim authority is valid for a maximum of 60 days from the date of issuance, or until a formal, permanent revision to the University Delegation of Authority policy is approved, whichever occurs first.
  • Fiduciary exception safeguard: Under no circumstances shall these non-production configurations be pushed to or replicated within the Oracle Financials or Jaggaer production environments without a signed, permanent policy update.

3. Compliance & Reconciliation Audit

The Analytics Manager shall maintain a weekly, automated configuration log verifying that no live transactional data or production workflows are impacted by these interim settings. All UAT test scripts executing under these thresholds must be archived for post-testing compliance verification by FAD Quality Assurance teams.

APPROVED BY: Office of the Controller, Harvard University

Control note: This memo is intentionally limited to non-production UAT logic. It does not authorize production approval-threshold changes, live spend routing changes, or any bypass of approved fiduciary controls.

Red Item Rule Protocol

Portfolio Governance Violations Closed by This Package

  • Exceeded red threshold: corrected from five red items to three active red items after formal downgrades.
  • Contradictory triggers: each red item now has an owner, dated action, and decision gate.
  • Executive overload: portal and AI are removed from red-level senior attention and managed through technical lanes.
  • Escalation status: Steering Committee protocol is now active and visible.

Best-Practice Operating Rules

  • Red item limit: no more than three active red flags without Steering Committee escalation.
  • Decision memo standard: every red item needs owner, options, pro/con, risk, and consequence if deferred.
  • Calendar discipline: convert decisions into dated holds, not informal follow-ups.
  • Evidence first: lead with cycle time, exception rate, adoption, and value-at-risk.
