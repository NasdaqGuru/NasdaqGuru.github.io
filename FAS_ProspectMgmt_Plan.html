Start with one trusted Prospect Action Queue — not a complex AI model.

The first useful product is a weekly table that says: which prospect needs action, why the system flagged them, what evidence supports the flag, who owns the decision, what the next step is, and whether the CRM was updated. AI should help assemble and summarize evidence; people still approve strategy.

View detailed breakdownOpen details

1. Standardize the fields

Agree on the minimum data needed to make an action decision: owner, stage, capacity, affinity, last move, next move, proposal, and exception flags.

2. Build rules before AI

Create transparent flags such as stale strategy, missing next step, no owner, proposal blocker, duplicate risk, and high-capacity inactive.

3. Add AI for draft support

Use AI to write brief drafts, summarize evidence, find missing fields, and prepare officer questions. Do not let AI create final strategy alone.

4. Close the loop

Every output must end with a CRM action: update, assign, research, hold, escalate, or schedule next officer move.
