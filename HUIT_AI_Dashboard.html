AI Program Executive Summary — May 2026

Portfolio is on track: 7,422 active users across 6 platforms, 47.3% license utilization, and annual spend of $2.41M — $190K favorable vs. the $2.6M budget. Cost per active user decreased from $367 to $325 over FY2026 as adoption grew without proportional license increases.
Claude Enterprise growing fastest: Monthly active users increased 28% over 90 days, driven by HUIT Finance, FAS, and HMS adoption of API-connected workflows. Utilization rate (77.5%) is the highest in the portfolio.
M365 Copilot underutilization requires action: Only 35.6% of the 11,800-license Copilot fleet is actively used monthly. A targeted enablement campaign and use-case library are planned for Q3 to increase adoption across administrative units.
3 pilot programs progressing: Perplexity Enterprise, NotebookLM Teams, and a custom Claude-API research assistant are in structured pilots. Evaluation frameworks and governance reviews are in progress for all three.

Financial Position — FY2026

The HUIT AI Enablement program manages approximately $2.41M in annual vendor spend across 6 enterprise AI platforms, funded through a combination of central HUIT allocation and school/unit cost recovery billing. All billing is tracked by assigned license, reconciled monthly in Oracle, and distributed to schools via a transparent cost model.
