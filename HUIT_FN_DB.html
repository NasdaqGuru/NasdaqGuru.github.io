Executive Summary

April 2026 — Key Findings

Infrastructure overspent by $162K (+9%) in April, driven by unplanned cloud storage expansion supporting the enterprise data migration project. A formal corrective action plan is under review with Infrastructure leadership; contract renegotiation with AWS anticipated to yield $80K–$120K in savings in H2.
YTD spend of $18.2M is $310K favorable to budget. Favorable performance in Cybersecurity ($53K) and PMO ($32K) partially offsets the Infrastructure variance. This cushion provides flexibility for H2 capital investments in security tooling and AI-enabled service management.
Full-year forecast revised down to $55.9M (−$0.5M vs. original $56.4M budget), reflecting vendor contract renegotiations completed in Q2 and a 60-day delay in the ERP modernization project.
28 open positions across HUIT (8.7% vacancy rate) create a $1.4M personnel savings offset. Hiring freeze exceptions approved for 6 critical cybersecurity and data engineering roles — expected to fill by Q1 FY2027.

April 2026 Variance Analysis — Executive Narrative

Overall: April actuals of $4.7M were $162K (3.6%) over budget, driven entirely by Infrastructure. All other departments came in on or under budget.

Infrastructure (+$162K / +9.0%): Cloud storage overrun tied to the Enterprise Data Migration project scope expansion approved in March. AWS consumption 28% above contracted baseline. Mitigation: contract amendment and reserved instance renegotiation in progress, expected to yield $80–120K H2 savings.

Cybersecurity (−$53K / −5.9% favorable): Two senior analyst positions remain unfilled (day 55 and day 90). Personnel savings partially offset by $12K incremental managed detection tooling renewal.
