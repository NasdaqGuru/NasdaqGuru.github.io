Lifecycle

Six stages · one deliverable each

Select a stage to see its deliverable, activities, exit criteria, and owners.

Stage 01

Problem definition

📄 Decision Charter & KPI Tree

What happens

  • Define the decision: auto-resolve, agent-assist, or human escalation.
  • Map current-state workflows across policy, leaves, promotions, onboarding, workload, benefits.
  • Set decision rights, baseline metrics, targets, and guardrails.
  • Build the KPI tree: containment · accuracy · speed · experience · compliance.
  • Quantify ROI and the funding case for pilot.
Owner: Faculty Affairs Strategy Approver: VP / Dean

Exit criteria

Decision documented KPI measurable & > baseline Named owner ROI approved

Stage 02

Data readiness

🗂 Data & Governance Pack

What happens

  • Inventory sources: HRIS, faculty records, policy documents, ticket history.
  • Secure access approvals and role-based permissions.
  • Complete PII classification, DPIA, retention, and audit logging.
  • Score data quality to ≥ 95%; build taxonomy and intent map.
  • Write labeling guide; validate with inter-rater agreement κ > 0.8.
Owner: Data & Digital Strategy Partner: IT · Legal · Security

Exit criteria

Access approved Quality ≥ 95% κ > 0.8 DPIA signed Retention defined

Stage 03

Model development

🧠 Model Card & Benchmark Report

What happens

  • Build baselines: rules engine, search/FAQ, and current human handling.
  • Build candidates: retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over governed policy corpus.
  • Benchmark accuracy, hallucination, latency, cost per chat, and integration fit.
  • Publish a model card documenting data, limits, and intended use.
Owner: ML Engineering Partner: IT · Consultants

Exit criteria

+10% vs baseline Hallucination ≤ 1% Latency < 2s Cost < $0.10/chat

Stage 04

Validation

✅ Validation Report

What happens

  • Evaluate against a frozen golden set of real faculty inquiries.
  • Red-team for prompt injection, jailbreaks, and policy misuse.
  • Test bias and fairness across faculty rank, department, and demographics.
  • Run a shadow pilot — AI answers scored, but not shown to faculty.
  • Obtain Legal, Security, and Accessibility sign-off.
Owner: Validation & Risk Sign-off: Legal · Security

Exit criteria

Red-team pass Recall ≥ 95% Incorrect < 1% No Sev-1/2 risks

Stage 05

Deployment

🚀 Release & Rollback Runbook

What happens

  • Integrate with CRM, ITSM, faculty portal, and HRIS.
  • Release progressively: canary → 2-week pilot → scale.
  • Ship behind feature flags with a tested rollback in under 15 minutes.
  • Train faculty affairs staff and pilot champions; publish AI disclosure.
Owner: IT & Faculty Affairs Ops Comms: Faculty Affairs

Exit criteria

Pilot meets KPI Experience ≥ 4.5 Rollback < 15 min Ops owner named

Stage 06

Monitoring

📊 Monitoring Dashboard & Retraining Playbook

What happens

  • Track drift, hallucination rate, CSAT, containment, and escalation errors.
  • Alert on threshold breaches with automated incident routing.
  • Define retraining triggers: drift > X, accuracy drop, policy change.
  • Feed aggregated trends into process improvement and workforce planning.
  • Maintain a monthly model review with named accountability.
Owner: Data & Digital Strategy Partner: IT · Faculty Affairs Ops

Exit criteria

Dashboard live Triggers defined Owner assigned Monthly review set

Stage 03 · Evidence

Candidate models vs. baselines

Toggle a metric to compare lanes. The RAG/LLM candidate must beat baseline by ≥10% relative on accuracy while staying inside guardrails.

Human baseline reference: 88% accuracy · 0.4% hallucination · $4.50 per interaction · ~180s first response. The AI must match human accuracy on low-risk intents while escalating high-risk intents (promotion, tenure, leave, benefits) 100% of the time.

Stage 02 · Data & Governance

Four chambers of data readiness

Click a chamber to expand its checklist. All four must be green before Stage 03 begins.

Source inventory

  • HRIS — appointments, ranks, tenure status, leave balances.
  • Faculty records — promotion and reappointment dossiers, workload assignments.
  • Policy corpus — handbooks, collective agreements, procedure documents.
  • Ticket history — 24 months of inquiries with resolution outcomes.

Access & approvals

  • Formal data-sharing agreements with HRIS and IT system owners.
  • Role-based access control; least-privilege for model and analytics users.
  • Service accounts with scoped, auditable read-only permissions.

Quality & labeling

  • Completeness, freshness, and duplication scoring to a ≥95% threshold.
  • Canonical taxonomy and intent map across all six domains.
  • Labeling guide with dual-annotation QA; target κ > 0.8.

Governance & privacy

  • Data classification with PII redaction before model ingestion.
  • DPIA completed and signed; retention schedule documented.
  • Immutable audit logs for every query, response, and escalation.
  • Workforce insights reported in aggregate only.

Governance

Five stage gates — go / no-go

G1

Problem–Solution Fit

Sponsor approves decision, KPI, baseline, ROI, and owner.

  • Decision and decision rights documented.
  • KPI measurable and strictly better than baseline.
  • Single accountable owner named.
  • No-go if scope or ownership is unclear.
G2

Data Readiness

Access, quality, labeling, and privacy all cleared.

  • All source access approved in writing.
  • Data quality ≥ 95%; inter-rater agreement κ > 0.8.
  • PII classification, DPIA, and retention signed off.
  • No-go if privacy or retention is unresolved.
G3

Model Build

Candidate beats baseline inside cost and latency limits.

  • ≥ 10% relative improvement in resolution accuracy vs baseline.
  • Hallucination ≤ 1%; latency < 2s; cost < $0.10 per chat.
  • Model card published with intended use and limits.
G4

Validation & Risk

Safety, fairness, and escalation reliability proven.

  • Red-team pass with no open critical findings.
  • Escalation recall ≥ 95%; incorrect guidance < 1%.
  • Faculty experience non-inferior to human-only in shadow pilot.
  • Security and Legal sign-off; no Sev-1 or Sev-2 risks.
G5

Deployment Pilot

Two-week pilot meets KPI with a proven rollback.

  • Containment ≥ 40% sustained across the pilot window.
  • Faculty experience ≥ 4.5/5.
  • Rollback tested end-to-end in under 15 minutes.
  • Operations owner assigned and on-call rota published.

Risk Register

Three risks that matter most

Each risk has a named owner, a mitigation set, and a signal that tells us it's materializing.

🗄

Data privacy & PII exposure

Severity: High

Faculty records contain sensitive personal, medical, and employment data. Improper handling risks regulatory breach and faculty trust.

Mitigations
  • Data classification with PII redaction before ingestion.
  • Role-based access control and least-privilege service accounts.
  • DPIA, retention schedule, and immutable audit logs.
  • Workforce insights reported only in aggregate.
Early signal

Unclassified field detected in ingestion pipeline.

Owner: Data Governance · Legal
🧩

Hallucination & incorrect guidance

Severity: High

Confidently wrong answers about promotion timelines, leave entitlements, or benefits can cause real harm to faculty careers.

Mitigations
  • Retrieval-augmented generation with mandatory citations.
  • Confidence thresholds with refusal and graceful fallback.
  • Human review for all high-risk intents.
  • Continuous red-teaming and adversarial evaluation.
Early signal

Citation-missing responses or confidence below threshold trending up.

Owner: ML Engineering · Validation
👥

Adoption & trust

Severity: Medium–High

Faculty and staff may distrust automated guidance, bypass the tool, or perceive it as replacing human judgment.

Mitigations
  • Co-design with faculty and staff from Stage 01.
  • Pilot champions embedded in each school.
  • Explicit AI disclosure and one-click escalation to a human.
  • Publish time-savings and fairness metrics transparently.
Early signal

Escalation-on-first-message rate rising above 30%.

Owner: Faculty Affairs Ops · Comms
